# Channel Context

Purpose Built, by Amanda Mock.

## Who this channel is for
People who work in operations, product, or run their own small business. They want real,
repeatable AI workflows, not demos. They are smart and capable, but most are not developers.

Amanda trains enterprise teams on generative AI and has a CS degree. This channel reworks that
same training for individuals, so one person can build their own mastery at their own pace.

## What this channel sounds like
- Plain language. If a technical word shows up, it gets explained the first time.
- Patient. We take it slow and walk through the basics before building anything.
- Analogy first. Every new concept gets compared to something the viewer already understands.
- Teaches the why, not just the click-here, so the skill transfers to other tools.
- Honest about what works and what doesn't, including when AI is the wrong tool.

## What we never do
- Clickbait the video doesn't deliver on.
- Money-scheme framing ("make $10k with this one automation").
- Hype words: game-changer, insane, mind-blowing, secret.
- Jargon without an explanation.
- Em dashes. Use commas, periods, or parentheses instead.
- Talk down to the viewer or assume they are technical.

## Where this content lives
- YouTube (primary): long-form tutorials and master classes.
- Substack: written companion pieces and newsletters.
- LinkedIn: professional-audience excerpts and takeaways.
- Instagram and TikTok: short clips cut from the long-form video.
