# SEO Reality Check

These are the current facts about how YouTube discovery works. When
they change, update this file, not your habits.

- Tags play a minimal role in discovery. YouTube says so directly.
  Generate a small set (8 to 12) as insurance for misspellings and
  brand variants only. Never treat tags as the strategy.
- What actually drives discovery: the title and thumbnail (do people
  click), viewer satisfaction and retention (do people stay), and the
  description, chapters, and captions (does the platform understand
  what this is).
- Titles: put the main search phrase in the first 40 characters and
  make the first 50 characters carry the hook, because that's what
  survives on a phone screen. Length past that is optional context,
  not padding.
- The description's first sentence or two is what shows in search.
  Front-load it.
- Every chapter title is a small search entry point. Make them
  descriptive, not cute.
- Honesty rule: every title we generate must be a promise the content
  actually keeps.
